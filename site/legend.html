Reference page for the adult. Every icon, color and box on the card is explained here. The card only shows the creature name and the move names.

Front and back

The front has the name, the image, the types, the health, the attributes, the moves and the resistances. The back has only the experience track, with 70 boxes. Each A4 sheet has 4 cards: the front sheet and the back sheet come out one after the other, and must be printed on both sides of the same sheet.

Types (colored disc)

Each type has a color and an icon. The small discs sit at the left end of the name bar.

Resistances (chips)

Each chip shows a type and its effect on the creature. They sit in the bottom right corner of the image, over the art. The card shows up to 3 chips, vulnerable first, then immune, then resistant. The other resistances of the species are in the chart below.

VulnerableDouble damage (×2).
ImmuneZero damage.
ResistantHalf the damage, rounded up.

Attributes

Each attribute has an icon and a row of boxes. A filled box is a point the creature is born with (base). The child fills boxes with stars up to the limit (slots). On evolved forms, the boxes come out empty: the value is inherited from the previous form on evolution.

StrengthAdded to the damage of physical moves.
EnergyAdded to the damage of elemental moves.
AgilityChance to dodge (d10 against this value).
DefenseReduces the damage taken.

Moves

Each move has a square (unlock mark), the move icon, the name, the die notation, a "+" and the icon of the attribute added to the sum: Strength for a physical move and Energy for an elemental move. Then come the effect (if any), the area mark (if any) and the charge dots. A support move deals no damage: it has no die and no attribute, only the effect. Move 1 of every card is always unlimited.

Empty squareMove not unlocked yet. The child checks the square when unlocking it.
Checked squareMove unlocked.
d4d44-sided die (average 2.5).
d6d66-sided die (average 3.5).
d8d88-sided die (average 4.5).
d12d1212-sided die (average 6.5). Only on a final form or a rare species.

Charges (dots)

Each dot is one use of the move. A move without dots is unlimited. Uses come back at the Healing Center. Spent: dot with an X.

3 dots3 uses.
2 dots2 uses.
1 dotOnly 1 use.
Dot with an XUse already spent.
No dotsUnlimited: use it as many times as you like.

Move effects (tokens)

A move has at most one effect. The effect becomes a token (marker) placed next to the creature and does not stack: if it is already active, nothing changes. All of them are removed at the end of the fight. Negative ones only apply if the move hits.

Weakness (gray, arrow down)On the target: the target's moves deal 2 less damage.
Poison (purple, drop)On the target: loses 1 heart at the end of each of its turns.
Paralysis (yellow, bolt)On the target: on its turn, roll a d10; on 1 to 4, it does not attack.
Sleep (blue, Z)On the target: loses its next turn. The token is removed after it loses the turn.
Swift (light blue, feather)On you: +3 Agility, up to a maximum of 8.
Shield (orange, shield)On you: the next move that hits you deals 0 damage. The token is removed after that.
Extra Strength (red, arrow up)On you: +2 damage on all your moves.
Heal (green, heart)On you: recovers 1d6 hearts.

Area (several targets)

The move hits every enemy. Roll the damage once; each target applies its own resistance and Defense, and the hit roll (d10) is rolled against each target.

AreaHits every enemy.

Experience track (back, 70 boxes)

The child crosses out one box for each victory. The marks work as below. The box with a double border is the next one.

SkillUnlocks one of the 4 moves. The first one comes unlocked.
Star+1 to an attribute of your choice, up to its slots.
Heart+1 health, automatic.
Strength+1 Strength, automatic.
Power+1 to the die result of a move already unlocked.
EvolutionMilestone that allows evolving. With the stone next to it, it needs the item. A species with several stones has a single mark, at position 40; the child picks the stone on reaching it.
Stone (item)Evolution that needs an item.

States

Each state has a shape and a color, not just a color. The printed card has no gray and no padlock.

FilledFull box or colored square.
EmptyThin outline, white background. Move not unlocked or mark not crossed out yet.
CheckedSquare with a stroke inside: move unlocked.
SpentUse dot with an X.
